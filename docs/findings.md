# Findings

Moved from the Q & A in Pathwise_notes.Rmd.

- **EPPO API v2:** host classes Major host, Host, Alternate, Wild/Weed, Experimental, Doubtful host, Non-host (no "Minor host"). Lineage: Kingdom Plantae > Class (Pinopsida; Angiospermae) > Category (e.g. Basal monocotyledons, Fabids) > Order > Family > Genus. Unknown codes return HTTP 400. https://eppo.cloud/api/docs is an unrelated company.
- **Lovdata:** 305 citable units. Bark beetles regulated as "Scolytidae spp. (ikke-europeiske arter)" (Vedlegg 2, nr. 10); alias file maps Scolytinae -> Scolytidae, conifers -> Coniferales/bartrær. Not named in the regulation: Xylella, Aromia, Lymantria, Dendroctonus, Polygraphus, Fusarium circinatum. Vedlegg 5A tariff numbers are an older format. Mattilsynet holds genus-level import data (§ 20, § 23a): a data request, not v1.
- **First runs:** ~30 s cold, seconds when cached. Most classes are `large`, as expected for upper-bound CN8 lines; imports from pest-present countries are often far lower (IPSXCA wood: 1.26 billion kg in total, 0.43 million kg from pest-present countries).

- **AGRLAX (Agrilus anxius, 2026-10-20):** hosts all Betula, present in CA and US. Before genus filtering, 90 % of "non-conifer wood from pest-present countries" was oak; with it, 47 t/year (mostly pallets) instead of 5.3 million kg. Lovdata: Vedlegg 1 nr. 2.1 (pest), Vedlegg 4A nr. 45, and the origin rule Vedlegg 3 nr. 11 (Betula from Canada and USA).
- **EPPO host commodities / pathways (2026-10-20):** GD lists host commodities per pest (categories: plants for planting, bulbs or tubers, seeds, pollen, cut flowers or branches, fruits or vegetables, stored products, non-squared wood, squared wood, bark, packaging material, manufactured articles, plant waste, soil/growing medium, agricultural machinery, all commodities), at genus level, only for EPPO/EU-listed pests (user guide p. 13-14, `docs/reference/eppo_gd_general_user-guide.pdf`). Not in API v2 (only a count, `infos$pathwayhost`) nor v1; shown as a server-rendered table on `https://gd.eppo.int/taxon/{code}/pathwayshosts`. Coverage in master_species.xlsx: 222 of 279 A1/A2 pests (80 %), 0 of 39 Alert-list pests. AGRLAX: plants for planting, squared wood, non-squared wood, bark, manufactured articles, packaging material, all Betula.
- **SSB labels vs customs tariff (2026-10-20):** SSB 08801 labels are shortened versions of the tariff wording. For 06029021 the SSB label ("Buksbom, drake-, laurbærtre, kamelia, kransgran, kristtorn, magnolia, palmer, trollhassel, vinterpryd") reads like a closed list, but the tariff says "trær og busker ikke nevnt ovenfor; draketre (Dracaena) og palmer (Palmae)". All 105 current lines are in the tariff (version 1.5).
- **Final sweep (2026-10-20):** 318 pests: 215 get pathways from EPPO host commodities, the rest from host plants. Remaining stops are genuine: XANTPD has no hosts in EPPO. EPPO host-commodity lists also use generic commodity groups (2WOOP woody plants, 2VEGP vegetable plants, 2FRUP fruit plants, 2ORNH herbaceous ornamental plants, 2HERP herbaceous plants). SSB label for 4403 23/4407 12 says "gran" (spruce) but the tariff covers Abies and Picea.
- **AGRLAX with EPPO host commodities and tariff wording (2026-10-20, worked example in `docs/vignette.md`):** plants for planting 35.3 million kg/year (large; 43 % is 06029021, all trees and shrubs n.e.s.), of which 949 kg from CA/US; wood 291.9 million kg/year (large; pallets, broadleaved firewood and prefabricated houses dominate), of which 49 t from CA/US. Only six SSB 08801 lines are birch-specific (4403 95/96, older 4403 99 01/02, 4407 96); birch veneer (4408 90 03) and birch-faced plywood (4412 32 01, 4412 33 01) are outside the scope. 17 Lovdata units match (2 name the pest, 5 origin rules).
- **Posit Connect Cloud (2026-10-20):** `rsconnect::deployApp(envVars = ...)` delivers the keys: the deployed start page loads (it would stop at `check_secrets()` otherwise). The bundle is 31 files, 113 packages, R 4.5.3. `rmarkdown` is not in the project library.
- **Pest-present countries only (decision 52, 2026-10-20):** AGRLAX: plants for planting 949 kg/year (small), wood 49 144 kg/year (small; pallets 66 %, crates 29 %). IPSXTY: 44 present countries incl. Norway, 43 origins, plants 17.4 million kg (large), wood 435.2 million kg (large, Sweden 343 million kg/year). XYLEFA: 22 countries, plants 1.9 million kg (medium). Many small flows have a median of 0 over 10 years, so the CN8 line table and the write-up also give totals.
- **More host groups, pilot (2026-10-20, decisions 55-57):** SSB 08801 has 317 codes in 0603/0604, 0701-0709/0714, 0801-0810, cereal seed (såkorn) and 12 09 (såvare), all with M1 = kg; many fruit codes are worded "friske eller tørkede" and many vegetable codes are split by season. Results (pest-present countries only): Tuta absoluta food 23.7 million kg (large, 0702 tomatoes); ToBRFV food 43.3 million kg (tomatoes, sweet peppers), seeds 40 102 kg (vegetable seeds n.e.s.); Clavibacter sepedonicus food 5.1 million kg (medium, ware potatoes), no seed-potato imports from its countries; Phyllosticta citricarpa food 14.9 million kg (oranges, clementines, limes); Bactrocera dorsalis food 38.9 million kg over 162 codes; Rhagoletis pomonella apples from CA/US/MX median 0 kg; Ips calligraphus fresh branches 71 288 kg.
- **EPPO names for crops (2026-10-20):** EPPO uses hybrid and variety names for citrus (orange Citrus x aurantium var. sinensis, grapefruit C. x aurantium var. paradisi, clementine var. clementina, satsuma var. unshiu, tangerine var. tangerina, lemon C. x limon, limes C. x aurantiifolia and C. x latifolia); "Citrus sinensis", "C. paradisi", "Poncirus", "Dendranthema", "Pennisetum" (now Cenchrus) and "Prunus insititia" (now P. domestica subsp. insititia) are not EPPO names. The EPPO taxonomy endpoint includes the taxon itself as the last row; varieties are typed "Species".
- **Open points from the pilot:** EPPO "packaging material" for herbaceous hosts (ToBRFV) maps to wood pallets (51 million kg/year); mixed 0602 lines dominate plants for planting for potato and tomato pests; exotic hosts get several n.e.s. food lines. See open questions 15-17 in Pathwise_notes.Rmd.
- **Sweep after decisions 55-57 (2026-10-20):** all 318 test pests run through pipeline, Panel 1, graph and write-up without R errors (317 ok; XANTPD stops, no hosts in EPPO). Pathways computed: plants for planting 298 pests, wood 156, other plant parts 75, food and fodder 56, seeds 23; 3 pests keep a manual pathway (stored products or pollen only: FUSAAL, TROGGA; PSDMAK). One bug found and fixed on the way: the "Only the host?" text failed on an empty code table (pests without imports).
